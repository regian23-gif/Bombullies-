# Play Store Listing Guide

This guide walks you through creating an effective Google Play Store listing for your app, including descriptions, screenshots, and all required assets.

## Overview

Your Play Store listing is the first impression users have of your app. A well-crafted listing improves discoverability, increases downloads, and sets clear expectations for users.

## Prerequisites

Before starting your Play Store listing:

- ✅ Google Play Developer account ($25 one-time fee)
- ✅ Signed Android App Bundle (.aab) ready (see [GOOGLE_PLAY_SIGNED_BUNDLE_GUIDE.md](GOOGLE_PLAY_SIGNED_BUNDLE_GUIDE.md))
- ✅ High-quality screenshots from various devices
- ✅ App icon in required sizes
- ✅ Feature graphic prepared

## Required Assets Checklist

### App Icon

- **Size**: 512 x 512 pixels
- **Format**: 32-bit PNG with alpha transparency
- **Guidelines**:
  - Should be simple and recognizable
  - Avoid using text (it won't be readable at small sizes)
  - Match your in-app icon
  - Follow [Material Design icon principles](https://material.io/design/iconography)

### Feature Graphic

- **Size**: 1024 x 500 pixels
- **Format**: JPEG or 24-bit PNG (no alpha)
- **Guidelines**:
  - Showcases your app's key feature or brand
  - Should look good across different screen sizes
  - Avoid important content near edges
  - Don't include device frames
  - No text or logos over 30% of the graphic

### Phone Screenshots

- **Quantity**: Minimum 2, maximum 8
- **Dimensions**: 
  - Minimum: 320px on the short side
  - Maximum: 3840px on the long side
  - Maximum aspect ratio: 2:1
- **Recommended sizes**:
  - 1080 x 1920 (Portrait, most common)
  - 1920 x 1080 (Landscape)
- **Guidelines**:
  - Show actual app interface
  - Highlight key features
  - Use your best screenshots first
  - Consider localization
  - No device frames required (Google adds them automatically)

### 7-inch Tablet Screenshots (Optional)

- **Quantity**: Up to 8
- **Recommended size**: 1200 x 1920 or 1920 x 1200

### 10-inch Tablet Screenshots (Optional)

- **Quantity**: Up to 8
- **Recommended size**: 1600 x 2560 or 2560 x 1600

### Promo Video (Optional but Recommended)

- **Format**: YouTube video link
- **Length**: 30 seconds to 2 minutes recommended
- **Guidelines**:
  - Showcase app features
  - Keep it concise and engaging
  - Include captions for accessibility
  - Show actual app footage

## Store Listing Text Requirements

### App Name (Title)

- **Character limit**: 50 characters
- **Guidelines**:
  - Clear and descriptive
  - Include your brand name
  - Avoid keyword stuffing
  - Match your actual app name
  - Don't use ALL CAPS
- **Example**: "Bombullies - Fun Battle Game"

### Short Description

- **Character limit**: 80 characters
- **Guidelines**:
  - Concise value proposition
  - Hook users immediately
  - Highlight main benefit
  - No promotional language
- **Example**: "Join epic battles, collect characters, and compete with friends worldwide!"

### Full Description

- **Character limit**: 4000 characters
- **Guidelines**:
  - First 2-3 lines are critical (shown before "Read more")
  - Clearly explain what your app does
  - Highlight key features with bullet points
  - Include keywords naturally
  - Add calls to action
  - Mention any awards or recognitions
  - Update with each major release

### Full Description Template

```
[Strong opening hook - what makes your app special]

[2-3 sentences explaining what the app does]

KEY FEATURES:
• [Feature 1 - most important]
• [Feature 2]
• [Feature 3]
• [Feature 4]
• [Feature 5]

WHY USERS LOVE IT:
[Testimonial or key benefit]

LATEST UPDATES:
[Brief mention of new features]

[Call to action]

ABOUT US:
[Brief company/developer info]

SUPPORT:
For help or feedback, contact us at: [email]

PERMISSIONS:
[Explain why you need sensitive permissions]

Your privacy is important to us. Read our privacy policy: [link]
```

## Content Rating

You must complete a content rating questionnaire:

### Steps to Complete Content Rating

1. In Play Console, go to **Content rating**
2. Fill out the questionnaire honestly
3. Categories covered:
   - Violence
   - Sexual content
   - Profanity
   - Drugs, alcohol, tobacco
   - User interaction features
   - Data sharing practices
4. Submit and receive rating from multiple rating authorities (ESRB, PEGI, etc.)

### Common Ratings

- **Everyone**: Suitable for all ages
- **Everyone 10+**: Suitable for ages 10 and up
- **Teen**: Suitable for ages 13 and up
- **Mature 17+**: Suitable for ages 17 and up

## Contact Information

Required contact information:

### Email Address
- **Required**: Yes
- **Visible**: To users on Play Store
- **Example**: support@bombullies.com

### Website (Optional)
- **Format**: https://your-website.com
- **Purpose**: Users can learn more about your app

### Phone Number (Optional)
- **Visible**: Not shown to users
- **Purpose**: Google may contact you

### Privacy Policy URL (Required for apps that handle sensitive data)
- **Format**: https://your-website.com/privacy
- **Required if**: Your app handles personal or sensitive data

## Store Presence Configuration

### Countries and Regions

Select where your app will be available:
- **All countries**: Recommended for maximum reach
- **Specific countries**: If you have licensing restrictions
- **Exclude countries**: If needed for legal/compliance reasons

### Pricing

- **Free**: App is free to download
  - Can include in-app purchases
  - Can show ads
- **Paid**: Users pay once to download
  - Set price (minimum $0.99)
  - Cannot change from paid to free

### In-app Products

If your app has in-app purchases:
- Clearly describe what users will get
- Set appropriate prices
- Consider offering free trial or basic tier

## Localization

### Translating Your Listing

To maximize reach, translate your listing:

1. Go to **Store presence** → **Main store listing**
2. Click **Add language**
3. Translate:
   - App name
   - Short description
   - Full description
   - Screenshots with localized UI

### Priority Languages (by market size)

1. English
2. Spanish
3. Chinese (Simplified)
4. Hindi
5. Portuguese
6. Japanese
7. German
8. French
9. Korean
10. Italian

### Translation Tips

- Use native speakers or professional translation services
- Adapt content to cultural context (not just literal translation)
- Update all language versions when you update English version
- Consider localized screenshots showing translated UI

## SEO and Discoverability

### Keyword Optimization

- Research keywords users search for
- Include keywords naturally in title and description
- Don't keyword stuff
- Use Google Play's auto-suggest to find popular terms
- Tools: Google Keyword Planner, App Annie, Sensor Tower

### Best Practices for ASO (App Store Optimization)

1. **Title**: Include primary keyword
2. **Description**: Use keywords naturally in first 2 paragraphs
3. **Screenshots**: Show key features clearly
4. **Icon**: Make it distinctive and memorable
5. **Ratings**: Encourage satisfied users to rate
6. **Reviews**: Respond to reviews (builds trust)
7. **Updates**: Regular updates signal active development
8. **Promo video**: Include if possible

## Testing Your Listing

### Before Going Live

1. **Preview**: Use Play Console's preview feature
2. **Check all devices**: View on phone and tablet
3. **Test all languages**: Verify translations
4. **Review assets**: Ensure high quality on all screens
5. **Proofread**: Check for typos and errors
6. **Get feedback**: Show to others before publishing

### A/B Testing (Google Play Experiments)

Once published, you can test variations:
- Different icons
- Different screenshots
- Different description text
- Test up to 3 variations
- Google shows results after statistically significant data

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. ❌ Low-quality or irrelevant screenshots
2. ❌ Keyword stuffing in title or description
3. ❌ Misleading descriptions or screenshots
4. ❌ Poor quality app icon
5. ❌ Not responding to user reviews
6. ❌ Ignoring content rating questionnaire
7. ❌ Missing privacy policy (when required)
8. ❌ Not updating listing with new features
9. ❌ Using promotional language ("best app ever!")
10. ❌ Violating Google Play policies

## Google Play Policies to Follow

Ensure your listing complies with:

### Content Policies
- No impersonation
- No deceptive behavior
- No inappropriate content
- No intellectual property infringement
- No hate speech or violence

### User Data and Privacy
- Clear privacy policy if collecting data
- Transparent about permissions
- Comply with data protection laws (GDPR, COPPA, etc.)

### Monetization and Ads
- Clearly disclose in-app purchases
- Ads must not be deceptive
- Follow ad policy guidelines

## Post-Launch Optimization

### Monitor Performance

Track metrics in Play Console:
- **Installs**: Total and by source
- **Uninstalls**: Watch for issues
- **Ratings**: Monitor average rating
- **Reviews**: Read and respond
- **Crashes**: Fix issues quickly
- **Conversion rate**: Store listing visits → installs

### Iterate and Improve

1. Respond to reviews (shows you care)
2. Update screenshots with new features
3. Refresh description periodically
4. Test different listing variations
5. Monitor competitors' listings
6. Update with seasonal content
